1.The short version

You can use Promptly without giving us your name or your email. Your footage is uploaded so it can be edited, and it is sent to the transcription and AI services that do the editing. We do not sell your data, we do not use your videos to train AI models, and we do not run advertising on you. You can delete everything from the account screen.

2.Your anonymous account

The first time you open Promptly, the app creates an anonymous account for you. It has no name and no email — just an identifier held on your device and on our server, so your videos and your plan have somewhere to live.

If you later add an email address, it is linked to that same account. You do not get a second account, and adding an email does not retroactively attach your identity to anything you made before — it attaches it to the account those things were always on.

4.What happens to your video

When you render a video, it takes this path:

  • the clip is uploaded from your device to our storage on Amazon S3;
  • its audio is transcribed by Deepgram;
  • the transcript — not the video — is sent to Anthropic, which decides the cuts and writes the captions;
  • the render itself runs on Modal, which has access to the clip while it is working on it;
  • the finished video is stored and served back to you over Amazon CloudFront.

Your footage is not published anywhere by us, is not shown to other users, and is not used to train any model. Links to a finished render are unguessable but not secret — anyone you send one to can open it.

6.Analytics and diagnostics

We record which screens you open and which actions you take — a render started, a paywall shown, an upload that failed — against your account identifier. We use it to find where the app is losing or failing people. Automatic screen-view capture is switched off; the app sends only the specific events it means to send.

Crash reports include a stack trace, the app version and the device model. They do not include your footage.

7.Session replay

A small random sample of app sessions is recorded as a replay — currently 5% — so we can watch where the interface goes wrong. Text you type is masked, and images and video frames are masked, so a replay shows the shape of the screen rather than its content.

Whether a given session is recorded is decided at random on your device when the session starts. If you would rather never be recorded, write to us and we will exclude your account.

8.Payments

We never see or store your card number. Purchases made in the app are handled by Apple; web purchases are handled by RevenueCat and processed by Stripe. What reaches us is the fact of a purchase, which plan it is for, and whether it is still active.

10.Deleting your data

Delete account on the account screen removes, in one go:

  • every video job on your account, and the stored files behind them;
  • your chats;
  • your usage events;
  • your profile;
  • the sign-in account itself.

It is immediate and cannot be undone. Save anything you want to keep first. Crash diagnostics already sent to Sentry, and purchase records held by Apple, RevenueCat or Stripe, are outside that deletion — write to us if you need those removed too and we will pass the request on.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first, as described in the Terms.

11.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to some of the ways we use it. Write to support@usepromptly.app and we will act on it.

If you use Promptly anonymously and have never added an email, we cannot verify that an account is yours, so we may be unable to act on a request about it from outside the app. Deletion from inside the app always works.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context advertising.

12.Children

Promptly is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used Promptly, write to us and we will delete the account.

13.Changes

We will update this page when what we collect or who processes it changes, and update the date at the top. Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect.
