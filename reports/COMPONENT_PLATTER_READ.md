# The platter and what comes back from it

**Read-only, no spend. 12 agentic run records (all that exist), 8 with a
readable timeline. Reference: `control_distributions.json`, 375 family
placements over 10 videos.**

## The headline

> **35 components are offered. 3 have ever been picked. 31 never have.**
> **Six of the nine occasion groups have never produced a single placement.**

Total placements across every run ever: **6**.

| picked | n | offered under |
|---|---|---|
| StatCard | 3 | WHEN A NUMBER LANDS |
| PullQuote | 2 | WHEN SOMETHING IS NAMED OR REVEALED |
| StepDivider | 1 | WHEN TIME OR SEQUENCE IS THE STORY |

## List 1 — never picked, and the references use it constantly

| family | agent | reference |
|---|---|---|
| **text** | **0 (0.0%)** | **220 (58.7%)** |
| **cutaway** | **0 (0.0%)** | **60 (16.0%)** |
| card | 9 (90.0%) | 95 (25.3%) |
| cut | 1 (10.0%) | 0 |

**The reference corpus's single largest family is the one the agent has never
once placed.** And the platter explains why better than the agent does.

The reference's text placements are measured at **middle 61.4%**, **size medium
45%**, case split evenly (lower 31 / upper 30 / mixed 30). **No component on the
sheet has that shape.** The text-ish offerings are:

- `EditorialQuote` LARGE, `PullQuote` LARGE — whole-screen, not medium
- `DropBanner` / `DropCard` / `StickyNotes` — TOP-PINNED, not middle
- nine `caption:*` styles — FULL-WIDTH caption tracks, a different job

There is **no medium, middle-placed, plain text component**. The most common
thing in the reference corpus is not on the menu, so an agent reading the menu
faithfully cannot produce it. That is a platter-description finding, not an
agent-judgement finding.

Whole groups that have never fired: SOCIAL PROOF (7 components), STEPS OR ITEMS
ENUMERATED (6), CAPTIONED (9), REGION POINTING (2), SCREEN AS SUBJECT (2),
CLAIM/STAMP (1).

## List 2 — picked far more than the references use it

| | agent | reference | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **card** | **90.0%** | 25.3% | **3.6x** |
| StatCard alone | 50% of every placement ever | — | — |

**Card is 3.6x over-represented, and one component is half of everything the
agent has ever placed.** The platter lists StatCard under "WHEN A NUMBER LANDS"
— the easiest occasion to recognise in a talking-head transcript, because a
number is a token you can see in the words. The occasions that never fire are
the ones that need a judgement about what the footage SHOWS ("this comment
exists", "look at THIS thing on screen", "watch this get dragged into place")
rather than what the speaker SAID.

**The menu is organised by occasion, and the agent only picks the occasions it
can detect in the transcript.**

## What this read cannot say

- **n=6 placements.** Every proportion here rests on six events. The direction
  is stark enough to be worth reporting and the denominator is too small to size
  the effect; a second batch would change the numbers and probably not the shape.
- The reference distribution is FAMILY-level. Component-level comparison is
  offered-vs-picked only — the corpus does not say which component a reference
  text placement would have been.
- 4 of the 12 records are probes with no timeline; they contribute nothing and
  are excluded rather than counted as zeros.
