1.What Promptly is

Promptly is an AI video editor for iPhone. You record or upload your own footage, describe the edit you want, and Promptly transcribes the audio, decides the cuts and captions, and renders a finished short-form video you can save or share.

You must be at least 13 years old to use Promptly. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you may only use it with a parent or guardian's permission.

2.Your account

Promptly creates an anonymous account for you the first time you open the app, so you can edit without signing up. That account is what holds your videos, your chats and your plan.

You can add an email address at any time from the account screen, and you will be asked for one when you buy a subscription so the purchase can be restored later. Adding an email links it to the account you already have — it does not create a second one, and nothing you have already made is lost.

Keep your sign-in details to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account. If you use Promptly without ever adding an email and then lose the device, we have no way to identify you and no way to restore your videos.

Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy, in your local currency. They vary by country and can change over time; the price you are shown at the moment of purchase is the price that applies to that purchase.

4.Credit top-ups

If you run out mid-month you can buy a one-off credit pack. Packs are sold in sizes of 5, 10 and 20 videos — 50, 100 and 200 credits.

Top-up credits are a one-time purchase, not a subscription: they do not renew, they are not refunded when your plan's monthly allowance refreshes, and they remain on your account until they are used or the account is deleted.

Some plans are offered at an introductory price for a first period — for example a discounted first month or first year. 6.Cancelling

For purchases made in the app, cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Promptly. For web purchases, use the management link in your receipt email or write to us and we will send it.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end the period you have already paid for — your plan stays active, with its allowance, until that period runs out. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

7.Refunds

Purchases made in the app are refunded by Apple, not by us. We have no ability to issue, decline or reverse an App Store charge. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Purchases made on the web are processed through Stripe by our billing provider, and we can refund those. Write to support@usepromptly.app and we will handle it.

Nothing here limits refund rights you have under the consumer law where you live.

8.Referrals

You can share a referral code from the app. When three people install Promptly and enter your code, you receive seven days of Pro, added to your account automatically. Rewards are capped at 14 days in any 30-day period.

The reward is for genuine referrals of other people. Codes entered on your own device, and codes entered on a device that has already been referred, do not count. We may withhold or reverse rewards where a referral looks manufactured, and may close accounts used to farm them.

Referral days are a promotional credit. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

Promptly's cuts, captions and transcripts are produced by automated systems. They are frequently wrong in small ways — a misheard word, an early cut, a caption that misses a name. Review a video before you publish it. You are responsible for what you post.

Different people can get similar results from similar prompts, so we cannot promise your output is unique.
